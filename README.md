## 🎮 What Is Degauss?

Degauss is a game browser for your MiSTer FPGA system. It makes finding and launching your retro games incredibly fast and easy. Think of it as a beautiful, modern menu that sits on top of your existing game collection. It doesn't change how your games are stored or organized. It just presents them in a way that looks great on your CRT television and feels snappy to use.

If you have a MiSTer with games, folders, and artwork already set up, Degauss will instantly recognize everything. You don't need to move files, rename anything, or run special tools. It simply reads what's already on your SD card and shows it to you in a clean, attractive interface.

## 📂 How It Works

Degauss is built around one simple idea: your games are already organized the way you want them. So why force you to change anything?

When you open Degauss, it looks at your MiSTer SD card or USB drive. It sees the folders you have, the game files inside them, and any artwork or metadata that a scraper (like Skraper or MiSTer Scraper) has already added. It then builds a beautiful, fast interface around that existing structure.

### What You'll See

- **Game Lists:** Browse games by folder, just like you would in the standard MiSTer menu
- **Box Art:** See cover images for your games automatically, if artwork is present
- **Metadata:** View descriptions, release years, and other details that scrapers added
- **Search:** Find games quickly by typing part of the name
- **Favorites:** Mark games you play often for quick access

### What You Won't See

- **No Waiting:** Games and folders load instantly, even with thousands of files
- **No Confusing Menus:** Everything is straightforward and easy to understand
- **No Background Tasks:** Degauss doesn't scan, index, or do anything when you're not using it
